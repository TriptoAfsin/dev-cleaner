# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

Dev Cleaner runs on your computer and does not collect, store or share any
personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or
ads.

## What stays on your computer

Dev Cleaner looks at folders, processes and ports on your computer to show
what can be cleaned. That information is shown to you and never leaves your
computer. Your settings are saved in a local file in your user profile (or
next to the app, for the portable version).

## Network requests

Dev Cleaner makes only these requests, all to GitHub, and only while
"Check for updates automatically" is on or when you ask for them:

- **Update check:** reads the latest release of
  `github.com/TriptoAfsin/dev-cleaner` (from `api.github.com`, or from
  `github.com` and `raw.githubusercontent.com` when GitHub's API is busy).
- **Definitions:** downloads the list of cache locations from
  `raw.githubusercontent.com/TriptoAfsin/dev-cleaner`. This is data, not
  code.

These are plain downloads. They carry no identifier, account or information
about your computer beyond what any web request carries (such as your IP
address), which GitHub handles under the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
Dev Cleaner does not receive or keep any of it.

Links you open from the app (GitHub, release notes, the Microsoft Store) open
in your browser or the Store app and are covered by those sites' policies.

## Children

Dev Cleaner does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
